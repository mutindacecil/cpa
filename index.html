About Cavenor

Clarity in planning. Confidence in delivery.

Cavenor Project Advisory is a Kenya-focused project management consultancy concept dedicated to supporting better planning, stronger project controls and more accountable delivery across construction and infrastructure projects.

Our purpose

To bring structure, accountability and professional discipline to development projects—helping decision-makers see what needs to happen, understand what is happening and act with greater confidence.

Our mission

To advance effective project planning and responsible resource management through strategic, professional and innovative project management advisory.

Our vision

To grow into a trusted project advisory practice recognised for excellence in construction and infrastructure project delivery across Africa.

Our promise

Every project deserves a clear plan, responsible management and professional execution. Our approach puts those principles at the centre of project decision-making.

Our approach

A disciplined path from brief to close-out.

Good project outcomes depend on clear responsibilities, reliable information and timely decisions. Our proposed delivery framework is built around five connected stages.

01

Scope & assess

Understand the project brief, objectives, constraints, stakeholders and current position.

02

Plan & mobilise

Establish priorities, responsibilities, programme expectations and reporting arrangements.

03

Monitor & coordinate

Track progress, surface issues and support structured communication and decision-making.

04

Review & advise

Assess performance, risks and emerging variances, then identify practical actions.

05

Evaluate & close

Review outcomes, document lessons and support an orderly project close-out.
