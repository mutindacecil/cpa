About Cavenor

Independent advice. Disciplined delivery.

Cavenor Project Advisory Limited is being established in Kenya to support organisations planning, managing and improving construction and infrastructure projects. We bring a structured, independent perspective to project decisions—helping clients understand what needs to happen, where attention is required and how performance can be brought under control.

We are an advisory consultancy, not a construction contractor. Regulated professional services are undertaken or signed off by appropriately licensed professionals where required.

Our guiding principle

Every project deserves a clear plan, responsible management and professional execution. Our work is grounded in integrity, excellence, accountability, innovation, collaboration and sustainability.
