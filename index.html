Who we are

Better-managed projects begin with a clear view.

Cavenor Project Advisory Limited is a project management consultancy being established in Kenya to support organisations planning, managing and improving the delivery of construction and infrastructure projects.

We bring a structured, independent perspective to project decisions — helping clients understand what needs to happen, where attention is required and how performance can be brought under control.

Every project deserves a clear plan, responsible management and professional execution.
